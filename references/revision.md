# Revision and Audit

## Establish the contract

Record the requested audience, length, language, format, and degree of intervention. A line edit preserves structure. A rewrite may reorganize it. A request to shorten authorizes cutting repetition, not changing the position.

For a major structural rewrite, preserve a recoverable snapshot before moving or deleting material. Ask before changing a deliberate architecture when the request only calls for surgical edits.

## Preserve meaning

Track names, numbers, dates, citations, modal verbs, uncertainty, exclusions, and causal claims. After revising, compare the new text against the source claim by claim. De-AI rewrites often drift by turning “may” into “will,” “one factor” into “the cause,” or an unverified claim into a fact.

Do not invent examples, grievances, personal details, citations, or confidence to make the writing sound human.

## Cut in this order

1. metadiscourse and rhetorical openers
2. duplicate claims and echo sentences
3. defensive exchanges with imagined objections
4. announced summaries, roadmaps, and conclusions that repeat visible structure
5. synonym cycling and ornamental adjectives
6. motivation or sanity-check asides that support no later claim
7. names for one-use devices and statements for one-use observations

Keep a qualification when it changes the truth conditions. Keep a counterexample when it proves a negative claim. Keep attribution even when cutting aggressively.

## AI-tell pass

Remove sincerity intensifiers, superficial trailing `-ing` clauses, false ranges, decorative triples, empty significance claims, passive circumlocutions, and dramatic fragments. Do not replace them with slang or conversational filler.

When the user explicitly requires text that must not read as AI-written, use two independent checks when agents are available:

1. Give only the final text to a fresh reviewer and request a 0–100 AI-likelihood score plus specific suspect phrases.
2. Give the original and revision to a separate reviewer and request a claim-by-claim fact and modality comparison.

Revise from concrete findings. Do not self-certify that text is human-written.

## Manuscript audit

Review mathematical manuscripts at top-tier program-committee resolution. Report numbered, concrete issues with line numbers and quoted text. Prioritize correctness, self-containment, proof gaps, undefined terms, symbol collisions, unsupported novelty, and citation mismatch. Skip praise and summary.

Verify each reviewer claim before applying it. Reviewers make mistakes about standard facts and local notation.

## Release check

Validate the actual output format: Markdown renderer, LaTeX build, bibliography, cross-references, or website generator. In Coflat, a physical newline inside prose becomes a visible line break, so keep each prose paragraph on one physical line unless the break is intentional. A source-level review is incomplete when the rendered artifact can change semantics or layout.
