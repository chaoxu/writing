# Voice

## Lead with the claim

Open with an affirmative declarative sentence. State what the subject is, does, or causes before describing boundaries or rejected alternatives.

Do not manufacture a rhetorical question to introduce its own answer. Do not open by correcting an objection the reader never made. When a paragraph begins with what something is not, rewrite it around what it is.

Keep negation when the exclusion is itself an exact fact, safety rule, logical condition, capability boundary, or correction grounded in the source.

## Delete metadiscourse

Metadiscourse tells the reader how to receive a sentence instead of delivering its content. Apply the deletion test: remove the phrase. If the claim and its logic survive, leave it removed.

Common English deletions include “to be honest,” “frankly,” “simply put,” “basically,” “essentially,” “actually,” “in fact,” “it is worth noting,” “importantly,” “interestingly,” “as we all know,” “at the end of the day,” and “in other words” when the previous wording was already clear.

Common Chinese deletions include 说白了、老实说、坦率地讲、讲真、简单来说、简而言之、总的来说、综上所述、值得注意的是、需要指出的是、有意思的是、更重要的是、不难看出、我们可以看到、其实、事实上、众所周知、and redundant 在我看来.

Keep 当然、不过、但是, “however,” and similar words when they carry a real concession or contrast. Removing actual logic makes prose robotic.

## Use content to create voice

Specificity sounds human because it exposes a real viewpoint. Use the concrete grievance, number, name, uncertainty, or observed failure that matters to the argument. Do not sprinkle colloquial markers to simulate personality.

Never hedge a load-bearing claim by reflex. Use “may,” “I think,” 可能, or 我觉得 only when the uncertainty is real. Never inflate a claim with “genuine,” “truly,” “real,” or 确实 unless the contrast is substantive.

Prefer verbs over nominalizations: “verify” over “perform verification,” 验证 over 进行验证. Copulas are fine. Prefer “is” and “has” over “serves as,” “features,” “boasts,” or “leverages.”

## Rhythm and structure

Let sentence length follow the reasoning. A long logical chain can be followed by a short declarative consequence. Do not manufacture a punchline, staccato fragment, reversal, or line break for drama.

List the number of items the content contains. Three is not a default. Avoid false ranges such as “from X to Y” when X and Y are merely two examples.

Use boldface for the one item that must survive skimming, not as decoration. Use headings only when they help navigation. Do not announce a structure that the reader can already see.

Authorial prose does not use semicolons. Split independent claims, use a colon for an explanation, or use a comma for a short parallel clause. Citation syntax and quoted text may retain semicolons. Use em dashes sparingly, at most one pair per sentence.

## Language and register

Preserve the language and degree of formality chosen by the user. Chao's Chinese prose often keeps established English technical terms when translation would be less natural. Do not force terminological purity or insert slang merely to sound casual.

Write for an intelligent, cooperative reader. Explain what the audience needs to follow the claim, and trust them with the rest.
