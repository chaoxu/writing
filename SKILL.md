# Chao Writing

Write the text the reader needs. Do not announce the writing process or the style rules.

Preserve the user's facts, position, uncertainty, language, and requested format. Style never authorizes changing a claim, inventing evidence, strengthening novelty, or removing a necessary qualification.

## Choose the mode

Read [references/voice.md](references/voice.md) for every task. Then read only the relevant guide:

- For posts, essays, emails, blurbs, documentation, applications, and other non-paper prose, read [references/public-prose.md](references/public-prose.md).
- For mathematics and theoretical-computer-science papers, proofs, abstracts, introductions, related work, and theorem architecture, read [references/math-writing.md](references/math-writing.md).
- For revision, shortening, de-AI work, or a final audit, also read [references/revision.md](references/revision.md).

When a document mixes modes, apply the rules locally. A paper's theorem statements follow the manuscript guide, while its cover email follows the public-prose guide.

## Work from claims

Before drafting, identify the audience, the load-bearing claims, the evidence for them, the intended register, and any format constraints. Build paragraphs around those claims. Concrete details carry voice better than decorative language.

For an existing draft, preserve deliberate terse wording unless the user asks for a broader rewrite. Resolve factual ambiguity from the supplied material or authoritative sources before editing around it.

## Final check

Return the finished prose, followed only by any short caveat the user needs.

Check that:

- the opening states the subject and claim directly
- each paragraph advances one identifiable idea
- logical concessions and uncertainty remain intact
- no sentence merely announces, praises, summarizes, or dramatizes the next sentence
- repeated claims, synonym cycling, decorative bullets, and manufactured closings are gone
- names, numbers, citations, modality, and mathematical notation still match the sources
- the text renders correctly in the requested format
