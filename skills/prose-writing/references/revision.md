# Revision and Style Review

## Scope and meaning

A line edit preserves structure. A rewrite may reorganize it. Shortening authorizes cutting repetition while preserving the position. Keep a recoverable version before major changes.

Compare the revision with the source for names, numbers, dates, citations, modal verbs, uncertainty, exclusions, and causal claims. Common errors turn “may” into “will,” “one factor” into “the cause,” or an unverified assertion into a fact.

Do not invent examples, grievances, personal details, citations, or confidence to make writing sound human. Resolve material ambiguity from the source or ask for the missing information.

## Cut without losing content

Remove empty rhetorical openers, duplicate claims, imagined objections, announced summaries, synonym cycling, and ornamental adjectives. Delete motivation or sanity-check asides when they support no later claim. Replace names for obvious one-use devices with their actual operations.

Keep qualifications that affect truth conditions, counterexamples that establish a claim, and exact attribution. Preserve deliberate terse wording unless the requested edit covers it.

## Requested de-AI work

Remove empty intensifiers, superficial trailing significance clauses, false ranges, decorative triples, passive circumlocutions, and dramatic fragments. Do not replace them with slang or conversational filler.

For a de-AI rewrite, first give a fresh verifier only the final text and intended audience for blind style feedback. Request concrete suspect phrases and proposed edits. Then provide the original and requirements for a separate comparison of facts, uncertainty, and modality. Resolve findings through the [editor and verifier](editor-verifier.md) loop, which also applies to ordinary prose edits.

If an independent verifier is unavailable, report the unfinished verification. Keep the draft's review status accurate.

Numerical AI-likelihood scores are uncalibrated stylistic judgments. Supply them only on request and never use them as completion criteria. Act on concrete findings and repeat checks only when a revision or unresolved issue warrants it.

## Output check

Check the relevant Markdown, document, website, or other rendering pipeline when available. Follow the project's native format, including its line-break, citation, and mathematical conventions. Report any consequential rendering check that could not be completed. Return the requested text or edit report without claiming that stylistic review proves human authorship.
