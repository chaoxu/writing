# Drafting Prose

## Build around a position

Identify the sentence the piece depends on. State it with the confidence supported by the evidence. Each following paragraph should supply evidence, a consequence, a limitation, or a necessary transition.

Prefer a specific observation to a generic claim of importance. “The browser returned 502 while curl worked” carries more information than “network reliability is crucial.”

Documentation describes what a system does. Commit messages and release notes describe what changed. Match the explanation to the document's purpose.

## Openings and explanations

Begin with the subject, event, request, or decision. Replace “It is worth noting that our paper has now officially been published” with “Our paper is now published.” Retain the venue or date when it matters to the reader.

An essay may begin with a concrete episode when the episode supports the argument. Name the mechanism behind a generalization. “Verification is the bottleneck” should identify which claims fail verification and where.

Use contrast when both sides matter. State the central claim directly, then explain a necessary boundary once. Mixed Chinese and English terminology is acceptable when it reflects the audience's usage.

## Emails and short messages

Put the request, decision, or news in the first sentence. Include the context the recipient needs to act. Preserve warmth appropriate to the relationship. End when the recipient knows what to do.

## Essays, applications, and posts

Use headings when the argument makes a real turn. A heading names the content. General labels such as “Why This Matters” often defer the claim the heading could state.

Concrete admissions of uncertainty can matter to the author's position. Preserve them without spreading uncertainty to claims the author presents as settled. Applications should make supportable claims tied to the audience's actual criteria.

Close on the final substantive claim. Remove appended recaps, inspirational slogans, and manufactured punchlines that contribute no further content.
