# Prose Writing

State the claim directly and give the reader the evidence or context needed to understand it. Preserve the user's facts, position, language, uncertainty, and requested format. User instructions take precedence over these style defaults.

Read [references/voice.md](references/voice.md) for the shared prose rules. For a new draft or substantial rewrite, also read [references/prose.md](references/prose.md). For shortening, de-AI work, or a detailed revision, read [references/revision.md](references/revision.md). All guidance is bundled here.

For a small edit, inspect the affected passage and enough surrounding text to preserve meaning. For a new draft, identify the audience, the claim the piece depends on, the evidence, and the intended register. For a substantial rewrite, preserve a recoverable version and check the new text against the original.

Remove empty metadiscourse, invented objections, repetition, and decorative phrasing. Keep concessions and uncertainty when they carry meaning. Never invent details or confidence to make a passage sound more human.

Check the finished text's facts, names, numbers, citations, modality, and requested format. Use the available build or rendering tools when relevant. Requested de-AI reviews use concrete phrase-level feedback and a separate comparison against the source for meaning preservation. Ordinary prose edits do not require independent reviewers.

Deliver the finished prose. Add only the short explanation or unresolved question the user needs, unless a review or change report was requested. Editing does not itself authorize publication or sending a message.
